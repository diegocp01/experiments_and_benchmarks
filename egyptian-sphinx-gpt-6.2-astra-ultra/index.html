The Great Sphinx

Ancient sculptors carved the Sphinx directly into the limestone bedrock of the Giza Plateau. Its lion’s body and royal human head face east toward the rising sun.

Archaeological evidence places its carving in the Fourth Dynasty, likely during the reign of Khafre.
